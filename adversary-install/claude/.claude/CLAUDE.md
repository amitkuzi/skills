# Mandatory adversarial QA

After creating or materially changing any reviewable deliverable, automatically use the `adversary` skill before handoff. This includes code, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, PDFs, images, video, websites, designs, data outputs, and configuration. Do not apply it to simple factual answers or actions without a reviewable output.

Do not retry external, irreversible, costly, security-sensitive, or user-facing actions without explicit authorization. Follow the skill's minimum four-iteration, score-gated review-and-repair loop.
