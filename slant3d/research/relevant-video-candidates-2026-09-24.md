## Method and completeness

Every one of the 635 catalog rows was evaluated exactly once. The existing title-keyword label was not used as the decision rule. The review combined the catalog metadata with fresh first-party YouTube metadata from `youtubei/v1/next`: **634 descriptions**, **417 videos with displayed chapter markers**, and **626 videos exposing a transcript-panel endpoint**. Classification used the title, the official description, displayed chapter titles/timestamps, and transcript availability metadata. No full transcript was downloaded or retained.

The selection is intentionally conservative:

- **Definite:** the first-party title, description, or displayed chapters explicitly teach or demonstrate transferable model/design preparation practice.
- **Probable:** a product case study or a segment of a broader video appears likely to contain transferable practice, but deep viewing/caption review is still needed.
- **Exclude:** the available first-party metadata points mainly to business, news, printer operation, materials, marketing, or manufacturing capacity rather than changes to CAD/model preparation.

The official YouTube video description was used as screening evidence but is paraphrased into topic tags below. Chapter links are included where they materially support the decision. A topic tag is a routing note, not a validated best-practice claim.

## Access gaps and cautions

- Direct bulk watch-page requests began returning HTTP 429 / unusual-traffic pages, so that route was stopped. First-party `youtubei/v1/next` metadata then completed successfully for all 635 IDs; this interface is first-party but undocumented and can change.
- One video had no description in the returned metadata; 218 had no displayed chapter markers; 9 did not expose a transcript-panel endpoint.
- Transcript-panel endpoints were observable, but transcript retrieval returned `400 FAILED_PRECONDITION` in this environment. Caption text therefore was not used to promote ambiguous videos to definite. Those remain probable where appropriate.
- Hidden, deleted, private, unlisted, Shorts, and Live items remain outside the source catalog's stated scope.
- This file selects research candidates. It does not yet extract or validate Slant 3D's engineering claims.
- No definite dedicated tutorial on mesh repair, watertightness, manifold errors, or STL repair was found in the returned title/description/chapter metadata. That topic will need independent sources if it becomes a skill rule.
