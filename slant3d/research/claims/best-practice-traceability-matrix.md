## How to read the matrix

- **Corroborated:** independent evidence supports the governing principle, while the exact geometry and any values remain application dependent.
- **Conditional channel practice:** Slant 3D supplies a useful, timestamped design practice, but independent evidence only supports the surrounding DfAM discipline or need for testing.
- **Source hypothesis:** the channel proposes a mechanism or geometry that has not been independently established for general use. It must remain a proposal until toolpath or physical evidence supports it.

No dimension, clearance, angle, wall count, force, density, cycle count, batch size, or compensation in the channel claims is a universal default. Numerical examples must be named parameters tied to a stated process and validation plan.

## Use rule

When a compact skill rule is applied, cite the matching row, copy forward its conditions and conflicts, and identify the evidence that will move the result from proposal to verified. If a rule cannot be traced to one of these rows, keep it out of automatic modification and request a separately reviewed evidence update.
